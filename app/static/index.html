Plants

Touching squares of the same plant share one label. Use Move to drag a block somewhere else, Unmerge to label part of a block on its own, and Merge to join it back.

Keys: B brush, R rectangle, V move, S select, U unmerge, M merge, E eraser, Esc closes notes. Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y to redo. Blocks with a corner mark have notes.

On a touchscreen, one finger uses the tool and two fingers scroll and pinch-zoom the grid. With Select, tap a block to open its notes.

Tap a block to see and edit its notes. Drag to scroll, pinch to zoom. The layout is changed on a computer.
